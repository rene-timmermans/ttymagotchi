# ttymagotchi 0.3.2

First native package release of **ttymagotchi**, a fork of
[Termagotchi v0.2.0 by Eze Olea Figueroa](https://github.com/ezeoleaf/termagotchi).
The upstream history, attribution and MIT license are preserved.

- Five species with ten growth forms and random names.
- Forty-five individually drawn post-egg animal portraits, with a distinct muzzle, ears, legs, stance and tail for each species and age.
- Six Happiness expressions, visible neglect and death; consistent mood during care.
- Attached paw gestures for washing, brushing and diaper care; food held at the mouth.
- Species-specific idle behaviours: blinking, tail wags, ear twitches, sniffing and waddling; young pets wobble, neglected pets shed occasional tears, tired pets yawn.
- Eight interactive minigames, hygiene needs, health damage and sustained-care recovery.
- Discrete diaper accidents, slow brushing and one configurable clock for needs, age and growth; saved age survives speed changes and offline play.
- Detailed large-terminal portraits, common frame sizes per age and grounded silhouettes across all five species; compact fallback for small terminals.
- English, Dutch, French, Spanish and German; arrow/Enter status shortcuts and Escape navigation.
- Automatic import of an existing Termagotchi save without modifying the original.
- Complete PNG atlas, species sheets and a five-species facial-expression review.
- Live `--preview` mode to inspect every species, stage, mood and care animation without opening your save.

Native installers target **Linux x86_64**: Arch, Debian/Ubuntu and Fedora.
Also included: portable executable archive, source, packaging recipe and SHA-256
checksums. Start with `ttymagotchi`; use `--version` to check the installed version.

The v0.3.0/v0.3.1 candidates were not published as releases. The Debian build
certificate issue was fixed; publication was then held for this visual redesign.
